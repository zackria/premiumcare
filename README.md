# Dr. Sabhana Firthous - Professional Medical Website

A professional website for Dr. Sabhana Firthous, MBBS, showcasing medical services, credentials, and clinic information.

## 📋 Overview

This is a responsive, modern website built with HTML5, CSS3, and semantic web standards. It serves as a professional platform for Dr. Sabhana Firthous, a Senior Assistant Surgeon and Government Gazetted Medical Officer, to share credentials, services, and clinic information with patients and the public.

## ✨ Features

### Main Website (index.html)
- **Professional Profile**: Complete medical background, qualifications, and experience
- **Gazetted Officer Services**: Document attestation, medical certificates, and verification services
- **Credentials Section**: Professional registrations, certifications, and verified credentials
- **Experience Timeline**: Detailed career history and professional development
- **Core Competencies**: Key skills and expertise areas
- **Approach to Care**: Patient-centered care philosophy and values
- **Contact Section**: Multiple contact options including WhatsApp integration

### Clinic Page (clinic.html)
- **Clinic Information**: Baby's Clinic address, location, and directions
- **Operating Hours**: Schedule display with morning and evening consultations
- **Services Offered**: 8 core medical services with detailed descriptions
- **Clinic Gallery**: Photo gallery showcasing the clinic space (3 images)
- **Contact & Booking**: Easy appointment booking options

### Additional Pages
- **Privacy Policy**: Comprehensive privacy and data handling information
- **Responsive Design**: Fully mobile-optimized across all devices
