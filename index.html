Life Certificates

Life certificates may be issued or verified for eligible individuals, including pension-related purposes, after an in-person appearance.

  • Accepted photo identification
  • Pension Payment Order or pension details
  • Prescribed form and authority instructions

Acceptance is determined by the receiving bank, department or pension authority.

PAN Supporting Documents

Attestation for eligible identity and address documents connected to new PAN applications or correction requests where the PAN process permits it.

  • PAN application requirements
  • Original supporting documents
  • Clear photocopies

PAN issuance and correction remain with the authorized provider and Income Tax Department.

Address Verification

Eligible address-proof documents and declarations may be verified or attested when the receiving process accepts Gazetted Officer certification.

This does not replace a formal residence, nativity or domicile certificate issued by the competent government or revenue authority.

Medical Fitness Certificates

Certificates may be issued following an appropriate medical examination and review of relevant health information.

  • Employment or education
  • Sports, travel or administration
  • Return to work or duty

Additional tests or specialist evaluation may be recommended when medically necessary.

Medical Leave Certificates

Medical leave certification may be issued when clinically justified following consultation and assessment.

  • Date of examination
  • Medically advised rest period
  • Expected return-to-work date, where appropriate

Certificates require an appropriate clinical basis; retrospective requests depend on records and professional judgment.

Cause-of-Death Certification

A Medical Certificate of Cause of Death may be completed only in legally and medically appropriate circumstances, such as when the deceased was recently under the doctor’s care.

This is not the civil Death Certificate and cannot be provided for unidentified, suspicious, accidental or other medico-legal deaths outside the permitted process.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about document verification, medical certificates and visiting requirements.

What documents should I bring for Gazetted Officer attestation?

Bring your original government-issued photo identification, every original document requiring verification, clear photocopies and the latest form or instructions from the receiving organization. Additional supporting records may be required for a specific service.

Can Aadhaar or PAN approval be guaranteed after attestation?

No. Attestation or verification does not guarantee acceptance. Aadhaar decisions are made by UIDAI, while PAN issuance or correction is handled by the authorized PAN provider and Income Tax Department.

Is a medical examination required for fitness or leave certificates?

Yes. Medical fitness and leave certificates are issued only when supported by an appropriate consultation, examination, clinical findings and professional judgment. Certificates cannot be issued solely on request.

Which areas are served for attestation and medical certificates?

Assistance may be available for eligible residents of West Tambaram, East Tambaram, Tambaram Sanatorium, Selaiyur, Chitlapakkam, Chromepet, Perungalathur, Mudichur and nearby Chennai and Chengalpattu areas.

Is a Medical Certificate of Cause of Death the official Death Certificate?

No. A Medical Certificate of Cause of Death may be completed only in legally and medically appropriate circumstances. The official civil Death Certificate is issued by the appropriate Registrar of Births and Deaths after registration.

Should I confirm availability before visiting?

Yes. Contact the relevant location before visiting to confirm current availability, the required documents, whether an in-person appearance is needed and the latest format accepted by the receiving organization.
